In Fidelity, open Activity & Orders, set the date range, then Download. Pick several files at once to combine accounts or date ranges; overlapping rows are counted once. Files are read in this browser and are not uploaded. Not affiliated with Fidelity.

Transaction types

Each type in your file is counted as money in, money out, or left out. Types the page does not recognise are listed first. Your choices are remembered in this browser.

Which tax year deposits count for

IRA and HSA deposits made from January to April can count toward the previous year's limit, up to the April tax deadline. Deposits labelled prior year are moved automatically. Change any that are wrong; your choices are remembered in this browser.
